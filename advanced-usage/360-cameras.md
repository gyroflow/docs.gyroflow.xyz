# 🥽 360° cameras

360° cameras are not supported by Gyroflow and adding support is not planned at the moment.

Since Gyroflow 1.7.0, Gyroflow can read the motion data of some 360° cameras: Insta360 X5, Insta360 Antigravity A1, DJI Osmo 360 and DJI Avata 360. It can't play their 360° video files, and stabilizing stitched 360° video is still not supported.
